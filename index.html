Throw. Place. Destroy.

A first-person dice duel for two. One die, two boards of nine cells, and an arena that kicks back. Easy to learn in a single match, mean enough to want another.

One turn, three beats

How it plays

You sit at the table. The die drops into your gripper. What happens next is in your hand.

01

Throw

Grab the die, pull it past the line and let go. It lands in the bowl of a bass speaker, bounces, and the cone throws it about before it settles.

02

Place

Put it on your 3×3 board. Equal dice next to each other multiply — two fives are worth twenty, three are worth forty-five.

03

Destroy

Your die wipes out every die of the same value in your opponent's matching column. Build your score, or burn theirs.

Three ways to play

Modes

Same die, same boards, a different game each time. The host of an online match picks the mode.

Basic

Drop the die into a column. Equal dice in a column multiply. The classic duel.

FreeMode

Any free cell is yours. Equal dice and straights score in rows, columns and full diagonals.

Criss no Cross

Columns again — but rows score too. Every die now pulls in two directions.

Across the table

Play online

Name your game, wait for a friend, press Play. They sit across from you — and you watch their hand take the die.

  • Host a game and it shows up in everyone's list
  • No ports to open, no addresses to type
  • Ready up in the room, the host starts the match
  • A computer opponent for when nobody is around
